### Mabuhay! 🌿

I'm **John Carlo Ampunin**, a passionate **UI/UX Designer** and **Web Developer** from the Philippines 🇵🇭.

Design has always fascinated me — the way a well-crafted interface can make someone's life easier and more connected. My purpose? **Building digital experiences that feel natural and look beautiful.**

I love working in **Figma** and turning those designs into real, working websites with **React** and **TypeScript**.

I am very passionate about:

- 🎨 UI/UX Design & Prototyping
- 💻 Web Development & Frontend Engineering
- 🤟 Accessibility & Inclusive Design
- 📐 Clean code & pixel-perfect interfaces
